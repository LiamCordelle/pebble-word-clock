# Word Clock

Time, spelled out. A quiet letter grid with minute dots and a glanceable tap page
for your heart rate, steps, UV, weather, upcoming sun times and a second time zone.

Customise the preamble, bottom caption, light/dark ground and common data settings.
Health and weather availability depend on permissions and connection.

Category: Faces. Target: Pebble Time 2 (Emery).
